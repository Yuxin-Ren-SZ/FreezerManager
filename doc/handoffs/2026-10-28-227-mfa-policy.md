# #227 — Pure MFA replay and bounded-backoff policy

Worker: worker-2 (Codex). Parent: #179. Design: accepted ADR0002.
PR: [#235](https://github.com/Yuxin-Ren-SZ/FreezerManager/pull/235).

This slice adds pure policy helpers only. It does not enable runtime MFA replay
or durable guessing protection, persist state/proof, change SubmitMfa, or close
#179's deployment gate. Existing generic TOTP generation/verification callers
remain unchanged.

## Delivered behavior

- `totp_matching_counters` validates the fixed local 30-second / +/-1 / six-digit
  profile, canonical case-insensitive base32 secret and nonnegative time. Invalid
  submitted codes return no matches; malformed stored inputs throw fixed errors.
  Every window counter is evaluated with fixed-length `sodium_memcmp`, without
  first-match short circuiting or converting future counters back into seconds.
- `accepted_totp_step` accepts only when all matching nonnegative counters exceed
  the optional floor, consuming the maximum coincident match.
- `MfaPolicyState` is the pure projection of floor, failure count, deadline and
  observed-time high-water mark. User identity/enrollment generation remain
  storage-owned. Functions take fixed wall time and return proposed copies;
  caller-owned atomic persistence/auditing is required in later phases.
- Failures 1–4 have no delay; 5–16 delay 30 through 61,440 seconds; 17 saturates
  at 86,400 seconds without inactivity replenishment. Equality at the deadline
  is eligible. Success/unlock preserve the floor and never lower observed time.
  Blocked evaluation, corrupt persisted projections and deadline overflow fail
  closed. A consumed floor or nonzero count requires observed time; delayed
  states require the exact derived deadline.
